    About Me

    • Hi, my name is Ruilin Yao (姚瑞霖). I am a PhD student at Wuhan University of Technology and a joint PhD student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA). My research focuses on multimodal reasoning evaluation, visual grounding (referring expression comprehension), GUI grounding, and object detection/segmentation.
