    About Me

    • Hi, my name is Ruilin Yao (姚瑞霖). I am a PhD student at Wuhan University of Technology and a joint PhD student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA). I study how vision-language models perceive, ground, reason about, and act in complex visual environments. My current work focuses on multimodal reasoning and evaluation, visual and GUI grounding, referring expression comprehension, and uncertainty-aware active visual search. I also work on core visual perception problems, including object detection, segmentation, and remote sensing and hyperspectral image understanding.

    Research Interests

    • Vision-language models: multimodal reasoning, evaluation, and agents.
    • Grounding and interaction: visual grounding, GUI grounding, referring expression comprehension, and uncertainty-aware visual search.
    • Visual perception: object detection, segmentation, remote sensing, and hyperspectral image understanding.
